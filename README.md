# 📊 Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard
An interactive sales analytics dashboard built using **Power BI** to analyze mobile sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment preferences, product demand, and sales growth trends. The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support sales planning, customer understanding, and business decision-making.

## 📌 Project Overview

In today's competitive retail market, businesses need timely insights into sales performance, customer preferences, and market trends to make informed decisions.

This project analyzes mobile sales data and presents it through an interactive Power BI dashboard, enabling businesses to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify high-performing products, understand customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate year-over-year sales performance.
## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses often struggle to answer important questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which cities contribute the most revenue?
- Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
- How do sales perform throughout the year?
- How does current sales compare with the previous year?
- Which sales trends can support better business planning?

This dashboard addresses these business questions through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.
## 🎯 Business Objectives
The project aims to:
- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing mobile models.
- Understand customer purchasing preferences.
- Analyze payment method trends.
- Compare current sales with previous year performance.
- Support sales planning through trend analysis.
- Enable data-driven business decision-making.

 ## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Total sales reached approximately **769M**, with around **19K mobile products sold**.
- Digital payment methods such as **UPI**, **Debit Card**, and **Credit Card** were the most preferred transaction methods.
- Certain cities consistently contributed a larger share of total sales, highlighting strong regional demand.
- Some mobile models generated significantly higher sales, indicating greater customer preference.
- Monthly sales analysis revealed seasonal fluctuations and peak-performing periods.
- Customer ratings were generally positive, indicating satisfactory customer experience.
- Year-over-Year analysis showed stronger sales performance in 2023 compared with previous periods.
- Month-to-Date analysis enables continuous monitoring of sales performance throughout the month.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations can support future business growth:

- Increase inventory availability for high-demand mobile models.
- Focus promotional campaigns in high-performing cities to maximize sales opportunities.
- Offer targeted promotions during lower-performing months to improve sales consistency.
- Continue supporting digital payment options to enhance customer convenience.
- Monitor customer ratings regularly to identify opportunities for improving customer experience.
- Use historical sales trends for better sales forecasting and inventory planning.
